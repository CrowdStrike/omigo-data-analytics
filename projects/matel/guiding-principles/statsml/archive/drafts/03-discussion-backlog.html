PIPELINE FRAGILITY

48. Butterfly Effect in Data Pipelines

A single miscalculation, missed dedup, or data loss early in a complex pipeline multiplies through every downstream step — screwing up all results. Not a million-year phenomenon: like viral spread, one bad record can corrupt millions of outputs in days. The compounding is multiplicative, not additive.
FEATURE ENGINEERING

49. Good Transformations for Raw-to-Feature

Turning raw values into ML-ready features. Naive 0-1 normalization loses information when categories have distinct value ranges. Category-aware normalization: normalize within each category's own range so GBM cut-points see meaningful splits. Also: log transforms for skewed data, rank transforms for ordinals, binning strategies that preserve signal.

53. Psychology of Panic Selling vs Comfort Holding

Loss aversion: panic when price drops 10% (sell at bottom) but feel comfortable when it rises 10% (hold, don't take profit). The asymmetric emotional response that destroys returns. In reality: should rebalance both ways. Prospect theory, disposition effect, and why "buy low sell high" is psychologically impossible for most.

DARK PATTERNS

58. Hidden Fees & Price Obfuscation

How industries hide the true cost: stock trading with different execution prices across exchanges (PFOF, dark pools, spread markup disguised as "commission-free"), "free shipping" baked into item price, autopay "discount" that's really a penalty for manual payers, resort fees added after booking, "convenience" fees, subscription auto-renewals, drip pricing. The distribution of what customers THINK they pay vs what they ACTUALLY pay — and the gap is the business model.
FINE PRINT

59. Marketed vs Experienced — Reading the Fine Print

When headline numbers don't match real-world experience. 100Mbps (bits not Bytes = 12.5 MB/s actual). Phone "128GB storage" where 20-30GB is used by OS/services. Disk capacity using 1000-based GB vs 1024-based GiB (your "1TB" drive shows 931GB). Internet "up to" speeds where peak burst ≠ sustained throughput. 5G coverage maps vs actual 5G connection time. "5-year warranty" with conditions that narrow after year 1. Understanding the gap between spec-sheet numbers and daily experience — and where to look for the fine print.
MARKET STRUCTURE

60. Stock Price Splits vs Crypto Fractional Ownership

Stocks trade in whole units — a $3,000 share means you buy 1 or nothing (until the company splits). Crypto can be bought in any dollar amount — $5 of Bitcoin is fine. This structural difference changes trade-size distributions fundamentally: stocks cluster at integer multiples of share price (discrete), crypto is continuous. How this shows up in the data shape and why models built for one break on the other.
ORGANIZATIONAL

61. Corporate Research Confirmation Bias

Companies fund and promote only research that validates their products. An open-source tool may outperform the in-house solution, but internal research generates "proofs" claiming otherwise — and those get published. Negative results are buried. The distribution of "successful research" is artificially skewed toward confirming the business narrative, not toward truth. Publication bias driven by economic incentive rather than academic pressure.
