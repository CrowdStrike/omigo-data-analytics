Hindsight Bias

"I knew it all along" — after learning the outcome, your brain rewrites history to make it feel inevitable. You lose the ability to learn from surprises.

Memory Rewriting

Product feature underperforms. PM says "I had concerns from the beginning." Check Slack — they approved enthusiastically. Memory rewrites itself to be consistent with the known outcome.

In data: After seeing feature importance results, every analyst says "obviously income was #1" — but zero of five pre-analysis predictions were correct.

Real damage: Collapses genuine uncertainty into false inevitability. Makes people overconfident in their next prediction ("I'm usually right") when they were actually just rewriting memory.
Write predictions BEFORE you know the answer. Date them. When the outcome arrives, read what you actually thought — not what you now remember thinking.
