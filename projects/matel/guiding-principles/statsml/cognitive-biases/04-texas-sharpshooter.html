Texas Sharpshooter

Drawing the target after the bullets hit — subgroup mining without pre-registration

The Trap

Looking at results first, then defining the hypothesis to match. You didn't predict the pattern; you found it by scanning everything.

  • A/B test fails overall
  • Slice by 20 dimensions — "Women aged 35-44 in the Midwest show +12%!"
  • That's 1 of ~200 possible subgroups
  • At alpha=0.05, you expect 10 significant results BY CHANCE

Example: Town finds 8 cancer cases on one block — "contamination!" But with millions of blocks and random incidence, SOME block will have 8 purely by chance. The cluster is the target painted around random bullet holes.

Defense: Pre-register your hypothesis. If you didn't predict it BEFORE looking, it's exploratory — validate on holdout data before claiming discovery.
