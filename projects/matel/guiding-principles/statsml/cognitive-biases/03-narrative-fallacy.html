Narrative Fallacy

Post-hoc causal story building around noise — compelling but unpredictive

The Trap

Constructing a compelling causal story around noise because the brain cannot tolerate "it's random" as an explanation. The story is written AFTER the outcome — it explains nothing.

  • Model accuracy improved after adding "time_of_day"
  • Team writes: "users are more impulsive at night!"
  • Actual cause: a pipeline bug duplicating evening records
  • The story was beautiful and completely wrong

Example: Three burglaries on Elm Street in one month — "serial burglar pattern!" With 200 streets, some street WILL have 3 in a month by pure chance. The cluster is expected, not meaningful.

Defense: Before building a causal story: does the CI exclude zero? Would I have predicted this BEFORE the data? Could randomness alone produce this?
