Overconfidence in Small Samples

Premature conclusions from early results before the signal stabilizes

The Trap

Treating early results like the final answer. Your brain locks onto the first few data points and refuses to wait for enough evidence to actually know.

  • A/B test running 2 hours. 47 treatment, 39 control — +22%
  • PM: "Can we call it?"
  • CI is [-15%, +60%] — includes "treatment is harmful" as a real possibility
  • The number looks large, so the urge to stop early is strong

Example: New employee resolves a critical bug in week 1 — strong first impression. By week 8: missed 3 deadlines, caused 2 incidents. But the n=1 first observation anchored the narrative permanently.

Defense: Set sample size requirement BEFORE you start and don't peek. Early data is a movie trailer — exciting, misleading, and not the actual film.
