Clustering Illusion

Seeing meaningful patterns in randomness. Your brain insists clusters mean something, even when they're exactly what chance looks like. Random events clump — that's what random MEANS.

The "Hot Hand" and Random Clumps

Basketball player makes 4 shots in a row — "hot hand!" A 50% shooter will hit 4+ in a row regularly by pure chance. Spotify had to make shuffle LESS random because true randomness produces clusters that feel intentional.

In data: Three people on a 20-person team share a birthday month — "what are the odds?!" With 20 people, the odds of NO shared birth month are near zero. Clusters are guaranteed.

Real damage: Teams build interventions around random clusters. Resources chase patterns that won't replicate because they were never real.
Ask "how surprised would I be if this DIDN'T happen?" If clusters are expected by chance (and they almost always are), there's nothing to explain.
