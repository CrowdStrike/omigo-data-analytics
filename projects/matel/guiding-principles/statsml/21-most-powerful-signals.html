11. Reaction & Reflex Time

Millisecond-level hesitation and response latency — the signal that's nearly impossible to consciously mask. Used by short-video platforms to detect genuine interest, by implicit association tests to measure hidden bias, and by intelligence services to identify concealed associations via Stroop-like interference.
