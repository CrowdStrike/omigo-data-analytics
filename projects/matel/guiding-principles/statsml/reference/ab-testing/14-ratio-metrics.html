Ratio Metric Traps

Design Flaw — ARPU up! Because you lost your cheapest customers.

Denominator Composition Change Fakes Improvement
  • Treatment: ARPU $50 (1000 users). Control: ARPU $40 (1200 users). "ARPU +25%!" But: treatment LOST 200 low-spenders (churned from worse experience). Survivors are higher-spending.
  • Total revenue: Treatment $50K vs Control $48K. The "25% ARPU improvement" = 4% revenue + 17% customer loss.
  • Ratio goes up when denominator changes composition, not because anyone actually spent more.

Correct approach: Track BOTH ratio AND absolute totals. Check for differential attrition. Report total revenue not just per-user.

The tell: If treatment has fewer users AND higher per-user metric → survivorship in the metric itself.
