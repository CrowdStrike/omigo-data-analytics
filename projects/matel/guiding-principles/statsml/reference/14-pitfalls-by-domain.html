Domain-Specific Data Pitfalls

Every domain has unique data traps, misconceptions, and challenges that general ML knowledge doesn't prepare you for. 151 domains + 4 derived docs covered — each with real-world examples and visualizations.

The universal lesson: Every domain has data that LOOKS clean but encodes hidden structure (missingness semantics, survivorship, reporting incentives, measurement artifacts, feedback loops). Generic ML knowledge gets you to the table. Domain expertise tells you which chair is booby-trapped.
