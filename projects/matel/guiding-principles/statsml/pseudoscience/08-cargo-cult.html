Cargo Cult Statistics

Statistical rituals performed without understanding their requirements

The Rituals of Science Without the Substance
  • p-value without understanding: "p=0.04, it's significant!" But: what was the hypothesis? Was it pre-registered? How many tests were run? What's the effect size? What's the CI? Just reporting p<0.05 without context is a RITUAL, not reasoning.
  • R² without validation: "Our model has R²=0.92!" On TRAINING data? On test? On new data? R²=0.92 on training with 100 features and 100 data points = memorization, not learning. The high number FEELS like science but IS overfitting.
  • Confidence intervals without calibration: Report CI but never check: when you say "95% CI," does the true value fall within your interval 95% of the time? If not, your CIs are theater — they LOOK rigorous but are miscalibrated.
  • "Controlled for confounders": Threw 20 variables into a regression. "Controlled for age, income, education, ..." But: did you control for UNMEASURED confounders? Did you check for mediators vs confounders? Throwing variables into a regression is not "controlling for" them — it's a ritual that looks like careful analysis.

Richard Feynman's "Cargo Cult Science" (1974): Pacific islanders built fake runways, control towers, headphones from wood — hoping planes would land. They had the FORM of an airport without the FUNCTION. Similarly: researchers use the FORM of statistics (p-values, CIs, regression) without the FUNCTION (actually validating causal claims). The rituals look like science from outside but produce nothing.
