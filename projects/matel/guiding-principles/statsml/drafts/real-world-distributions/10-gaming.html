Gaming & Entertainment — Distribution Patterns

POWER LAW

Player Session Length (Power Law)

Most sessions very short (casual), few extremely long (hardcore).

  • Power law of engagement — bulk of sessions under 10 minutes
  • Why median is 5 min but mean is 45 min — extreme right tail pulls the average
  • How matchmaking suffers if you don't segment by session length
WHALE ECONOMICS

In-App Purchase Amount (Whale Distribution)

90% never pay, 8% spend small, 2% are "whales" spending thousands.

  • Extreme inequality — top 1% accounts for 50% of revenue
  • Why ARPU (average revenue per user) is misleading
  • Freemium model economics depend entirely on the tail
DESIGNED NORMAL

Matchmaking Rating (Elo/MMR)

Near-normal centered at 1200, slight right skew from skill ceiling, compression at extremes.

  • Designed to be normal — Elo system enforces bell-curve by construction
  • Rating inflation over time as new players enter at floor
  • How smurf accounts create a left-tail anomaly
U-SHAPED

Achievement Completion Rate

U-shaped (Beta): easy achievements near 100%, hard ones near 0%, few in middle.

  • Game designers create bimodal difficulty by intent
  • Middle-difficulty achievements are rare — most are trivial or extreme
  • Survivorship bias in completion data skews perception
NON-STATIONARY

Daily Active Users (Seasonal + Trend)

Normal-ish with weekend peaks, event spikes, and gradual decline after launch.

  • Seasonality — weekends and holidays drive engagement cycles
  • Event-driven spikes from updates, promotions, viral moments
  • Churn creates non-stationarity — why week-over-week is better than day-over-day
