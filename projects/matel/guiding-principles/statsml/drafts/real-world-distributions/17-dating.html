Dating Apps — Distribution Patterns

ASYMMETRIC BIMODAL

Like Rate (Market Failure Visible in Shape)

Men = broad distribution centered at 40-60% (swipe right on most). Women = tight spike at 4-10% (highly selective). The asymmetry creates a market failure: men's high rate overwhelms women → women become more selective → men compensate by swiping more → feedback loop. The shape proves the UX problem is structural.

  • Men: broad, centered 40-60% (low selectivity)
  • Women: tight spike at 4-10% (high selectivity)
  • Asymmetry creates positive feedback loop (market failure)
  • Shape proves UX problem is structural, not fixable by profiles
ZERO-INFLATED

Message Response Rate (It's Lead Gen, Not Dating)

85% at exactly 0 (no response ever), then among responses: geometric decay (most reply once, stop). Shape is statistically indistinguishable from cold sales outreach. Proves conversations are funnel metrics, not interpersonal. Platform is lead generation wearing a social mask.

  • 85% at exactly 0 = no response ever
  • Geometric decay among responses (most = one reply)
  • Shape identical to cold sales email response rates
  • Platform = lead generation system, not social platform
BIMODAL (ACT VS HOARD)

Match-to-Message Time (30% of Matches Are Validation Hoarding)

Spike within 5 minutes (excited/impulsive), rapid decay, rising mass at "never messaged" (~30%). Match ≠ interest — matches are collected as validation without intent to act. "Match rate" is a vanity metric — true conversion is 70% lower than the headline.

  • Spike at <5 min = excited/impulsive (will message)
  • ~30% = never message (match hoarding/validation)
  • Matching ≠ interest — collected as validation
  • True conversion 70% lower than reported match rates
GEOMETRIC + BUMP

Conversation Length (Interest Doesn't Build — It Randomly Collapses)

Geometric decay (constant per-message abandon probability), but with a bump at 15-25 messages = the "let's meet" transition point. Geometric body proves each message is independent flip — interest doesn't build. Bump = only survivors long enough to transition to real life.

  • Geometric body = constant abandon probability per message
  • Interest doesn't build — it randomly collapses
  • Bump at 15-25 messages = "let's meet" decision point
  • Below bump = entertainment, not dating
ASSUMPTION MISMATCH

Attractiveness Elo (Wrong Distributional Assumption Breaks Algorithm)

Platform assumes/expects Gaussian rating distribution. Actual engagement follows power law — top 10% receive 60%+ of all likes. The mismatch breaks the matching algorithm. Elo assumes each interaction is informative (Gaussian update), but rejection by top-10% carries no information — everyone gets rejected.

  • Platform assumes Gaussian (Elo system designed for it)
  • Reality: power law (top 10% = 60%+ of likes)
  • Mismatch = algorithm learns slowly (wrong update model)
  • Rejection from top user = no information (universal event)
