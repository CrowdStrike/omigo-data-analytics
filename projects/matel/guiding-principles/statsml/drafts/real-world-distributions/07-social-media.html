Social Media & Content — Distribution Patterns

POWER LAW + CUTOFF

Post Virality (Algorithmic Suppression Visible in Shape)

Most posts get 0-2 shares, power law decay for popular ones, but a hard cutoff where growth is throttled. The cutoff isn't natural — it's algorithmic suppression. Pure virality would be unbounded power law. The cutoff point reveals where the platform's circuit breaker kicks in.

  • Most posts: 0-2 shares (near-zero engagement)
  • Power law body for popular posts
  • Hard cutoff = platform's algorithmic circuit breaker
  • Cutoff point reverse-engineers content moderation thresholds
BIMODAL (ADDICTION SIGNATURE)

Inter-Post Intervals (No Moderate Usage Exists)

Spike at 1-5 minutes (scrolling/posting binge), dead zone, long tail of days/weeks (dormancy). No "moderate usage" exists. The bimodality is the signature of addiction design — the app wants you in the binge mode, and the gap = activation energy to pull you back.

  • Spike at 1-5 min = compulsive session (binge mode)
  • Long tail at days/weeks = dormancy
  • No moderate middle = addiction design working
  • Gap between modes = activation energy barrier
POWER LAW (PREFERENTIAL ATTACHMENT)

Follower Counts (Algorithm Amplifies Inequality)

99% of users have <1000 followers, power law tail with extreme outliers at millions. Preferential attachment — new followers go to people who already have followers. The exponent tells you exactly how winner-take-all the platform's algorithm is.

  • 99% have <1000 followers
  • Power law tail = preferential attachment (rich get richer)
  • Exponent measures how winner-take-all the algorithm is
  • Steeper exponent = more mid-tier creator suppression
TRIMODAL (ENGAGEMENT REQUIRES EXTREMES)

Comment Sentiment (Moderate Opinions Produce Silence)

Spike at very negative, mass at neutral/indifferent, spike at very positive. Nothing in between. Moderate opinions produce silence — engagement requires emotional activation. Platforms optimize for the extreme modes = polarization is a distribution feature, not a bug.

  • Spikes at negative and positive extremes
  • Broad mass at neutral (lurkers, not commenters)
  • Middle opinions → silence (no motivation to write)
  • Platform engagement optimization = polarization
PLATFORM-DEPENDENT SHAPE

Content Half-Life (Algorithm Architecture Made Visible)

Twitter = extremely left-skewed (half-life ~18 min). YouTube = right-skewed (weeks/months). TikTok = bimodal (dead fast OR algorithmically revived). The shape IS the recommendation architecture — feed-based = fast decay, search-based = long tail.

  • Twitter: left-skewed, half-life ~18 min (feed-based decay)
  • YouTube: right-skewed, half-life weeks (search/recommendation)
  • TikTok: bimodal (dead or revived by algorithm)
  • Shape = direct signature of recommendation architecture
