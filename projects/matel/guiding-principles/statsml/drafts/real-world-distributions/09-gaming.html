Gaming & Entertainment — Distribution Patterns

LOG-NORMAL + SPIKES

Session Length (Game Design Controls When You Stop)

Right-skewed hill (median ~25 min) with needle spikes at 20, 40, 60 minutes — match/round durations. Players don't choose when to stop, the game's structure decides. Between spikes nearly nobody quits (sunk cost + penalty). The distribution IS the retention architecture.

  • Spikes at 20, 40, 60 min = match/round boundaries
  • Between spikes = almost nobody quits (sunk cost + penalties)
  • Game designer controls exit points by controlling match length
  • Distribution shape = retention architecture made visible
ZERO-INFLATED PARETO

In-App Spending (Economy Funded by Addiction)

95% at exactly $0, then among spenders 90% spend <$20, but top 0.1% spend $10K+. The entire F2P economy is funded by compulsive spenders, not customers. Shape is structurally identical to gambling losses.

  • 95% at $0 — non-payers
  • Top 0.1% spend $10K+ (whales)
  • Shape identical to gambling loss distribution
  • "ARPU" averages addicts with non-spenders — meaningless
COMPRESSED NORMAL

Matchmaking Rating (The System Is Lying at the Extremes)

Gaussian in the middle, but mass piles up at Bronze floor (can't drop below) and hard wall near top. The compression hides information — a player at the floor could be anywhere from terrible to decent-but-new. True skill is only visible in the Gaussian middle.

  • Gaussian middle = system has real information
  • Mass piled at floor = can't drop below (info hidden)
  • Ceiling compression = top ranks are undifferentiated
  • Rating only meaningful in the middle band
DESIGNED POWER LAW

Loot Box Rewards (Engineered to Mimic Slot Machines)

Massive probability on common items (99%), geometric decay through tiers, isolated spike at legendary (~0.1%). Unlike natural power laws, these probabilities are hand-crafted to maximize spend. The gap between rare and legendary is deliberately widened for near-miss psychology.

  • 99% probability on common (filler items)
  • Geometric decay through rare tiers
  • Isolated spike at legendary = near-miss psychology
  • Probabilities chosen to maximize spend, not model reality
LEFT-TRUNCATED EXPONENTIAL

Time to First Kill (Map Design Readable from Shape)

Zero probability below ~3 seconds (spawn protection + physics), then exponential decay. The exponential proves encounters are memoryless (random movement). The truncation point reveals map design — tighter maps = shorter truncation = faster pace.

  • Zero below ~3 sec = spawn protection + physics constraint
  • Exponential body = encounters are memoryless (random movement)
  • Truncation point = map size/complexity metric
  • Tighter map = shorter truncation = faster game pace
