# Interesting Problems & Paradoxes

**Page type:** grid page (3-column card grid with a 2-tag pill footer per card, philosophy callouts above and below)
**HTML title tag:** Interesting Problems & Paradoxes

**Subtitle:** Famous results from probability, game theory, and combinatorics that are non-trivial to grasp at first encounter. Each one is explained with simplified real-life examples that build intuition from scratch.

## Callout (philosophy box, top)

**Why this section exists:** These are established mathematical results that working data scientists encounter repeatedly — in system design, pricing, auctions, security, testing — but whose mechanics feel wrong until you sit with them. Each page uses concrete everyday scenarios to make the concept click.
