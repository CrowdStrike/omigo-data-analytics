Birthday Paradox

Collisions happen far sooner than your intuition says.

How many people do you need in a room for a 50% chance that two share a birthday? Most people guess around 180. The real answer: 23.

Why does intuition fail? You're not asking "does someone share MY birthday" (that needs ~253 people). You're asking "does ANY pair match" — and with 23 people there are 253 possible pairs. Each pair is a separate chance for a collision to happen.

In data systems: "We only have 10,000 users, a random 32-bit session ID will never collide." But with 77,000 sessions, collision probability hits 50%. Systems break at scales people think are safe, because humans underestimate how fast pairs grow.

Lesson: Whenever you're thinking "that overlap/collision is too unlikely to worry about" — do the math. Pairs grow as n², not n. Rare events become likely much faster than you think.
