Will Rogers Phenomenon

Moving borderline cases between groups can improve both averages.

Your analytics team re-segments users: "medium" tier users near the bottom get reclassified as "low" tier. Next report shows average revenue went up in both tiers. The exec deck says "all segments grew!"

Nothing changed. Those borderline users were the worst in "medium" (pulling its average down) but better than the "low" average (pulling it up). Moving them improved both numbers with zero real change.

Lesson: When segment definitions change, don't compare old averages to new ones. The math guarantees "improvement" from reclassification alone.
