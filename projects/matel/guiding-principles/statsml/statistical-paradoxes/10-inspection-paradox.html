Inspection Paradox

You're more likely to observe things that last longer.

Your analytics dashboard says "average session length is 3 minutes" (computed from completed sessions). But when you sample users who are active right now, their average session is 14 minutes.

That's not a bug. If you drop a pin on a random moment in time, you're more likely to land inside a long session than a short one — the same way you're more likely to arrive at a bus stop during a long gap than a short gap. The "active now" sample over-represents long sessions.

Lesson: How you sample changes what you see. "Completed events" and "in-progress snapshots" give different answers — both correct, for different questions.
