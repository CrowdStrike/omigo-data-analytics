Multiple Comparisons

Test enough things and you'll find "winners" by pure chance.

Your marketing team tests 50 email subject lines against a control. Three show "statistically significant" lift at p<0.05. They declare three winners and roll them out.

But 50 tests × 5% false-positive rate = 2.5 expected false wins even when NO subject line is actually better. Those 3 "winners" are likely just noise that passed a threshold.

Lesson: The more things you test, the stricter your significance threshold must be. Use Bonferroni correction (p<0.05/n_tests) or FDR.
