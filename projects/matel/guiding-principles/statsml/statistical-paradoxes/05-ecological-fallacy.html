Ecological Fallacy

Group averages don't describe individuals.

You look at your user segments. The "High Spend" segment has 78% retention. The "Low Spend" segment has 40% retention. Easy conclusion: spending more = staying longer. Target the big spenders with retention offers.

But when you zoom in, within the high-spend group it's actually the small-but-frequent buyers (buying coffee daily) who stay at 95%. The big one-time buyers (one $500 purchase) churn at 35%. The segment average hid completely opposite behaviors inside.

Lesson: A segment-level stat does not apply to every individual in that segment. Always check within-group variation before making decisions.
