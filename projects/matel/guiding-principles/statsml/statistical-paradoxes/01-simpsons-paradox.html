Simpson's Paradox

A trend in every subgroup can reverse when you combine them.

Your team A/B tests a new checkout flow. The new version wins on mobile (3.0% vs 2.5%) and wins on desktop (12.0% vs 11.5%). Every segment says "ship it."

But overall conversion is lower for the new version. Why? 80% of the "new" group were mobile users (who convert less in general). Only 20% were desktop. The old version had the opposite split. So the new variant was tested on the harder audience, and the aggregate got dragged down.

Same numbers, opposite conclusion — depending on whether you look at parts or the whole.

Lesson: Always break results down by segment. An aggregate can hide — or invert — what's really happening.
