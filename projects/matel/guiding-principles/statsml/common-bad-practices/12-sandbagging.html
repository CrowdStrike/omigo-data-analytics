Sandbagging Estimates

Metric Manipulation — Estimate 8 weeks for 3-week work. Deliver in 5. 'Beat expectations by 37%!'

The Practice
  • Know it takes 3 weeks. Estimate 8. Deliver in 5.
  • "Beat expectations by 37%! Ahead of schedule!"
  • Consistent under-promising trains leadership to expect slow delivery. Actual velocity is permanently hidden behind inflated estimates.
The Compounding Effect
  • Sandbag consistently: leadership expects "8 weeks per project." Deliver in 5 — you look fast.
  • Someone who estimates honestly (3 weeks) and delivers in 3 looks "average."
  • Same speed, different perception. Sandbagging PENALIZES honest estimators by comparison.
