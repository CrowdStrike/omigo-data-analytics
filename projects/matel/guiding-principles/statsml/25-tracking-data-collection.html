Tracking Data Collection Methods

How your data actually gets collected — the mechanisms, who's watching, what they capture, and how much they can really see.

The uncomfortable truth: A single action — tapping a link, walking past a store, driving to work — gets captured by multiple independent systems simultaneously. No single tracker seems harmful alone. The danger is that someone can stitch them all together.
WEB

1. Server-Side Logging

Every time you visit a website, the server records your visit — your IP address, what page, what time, what device.

2. JavaScript Tags

Hidden scripts on websites that watch everything you do — where you click, how far you scroll, what you type.

3. Analytics Platforms

Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude — tools that track your entire journey through a website or app.

4. Ad Tracking Pixels

Invisible 1×1 images loaded from Facebook, Google, etc. that tell advertisers you visited a page or bought something.

ads invisible cross-site

5. Cookies

Small files websites store on your browser to remember you — and share that memory with ad companies across every site you visit.

web identity cross-site
ADVERTISING

6. Browser Fingerprinting

Identifying you without cookies — by combining your screen size, fonts, browser version, and dozens of other tiny details into a unique ID.

web covert no-consent

7. Mobile App SDKs

Code libraries buried inside apps that report everything you do back to analytics and ad companies.
