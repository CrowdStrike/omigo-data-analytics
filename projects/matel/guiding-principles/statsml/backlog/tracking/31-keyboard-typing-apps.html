Keyboard Apps (Gboard, SwiftKey)

Your phone keyboard sees every single word you type — messages, passwords, searches — and sends it back to Google or Microsoft.

What is it?

Third-party keyboard apps replace the built-in keyboard on your phone. Gboard (Google) and SwiftKey (Microsoft) are the most popular. They need to see everything you type to "predict" your next word. That means every text message, password, email, and search goes through their servers.
What does it collect?
  • Every word and sentence you type
  • How fast you type (typing rhythm)
  • Which words you delete and retype
  • Which apps you're typing in
  • Your custom dictionary and slang
  • Swipe patterns and autocorrect choices
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: "To improve predictions and autocorrect."

What they don't tell you: Your typing builds a profile of your interests, relationships, writing style, and emotional state. This feeds ad targeting. They know what you almost said but deleted.
How much can they see?

Everything. Every character. Every hesitation. Every deleted draft. The keyboard sits between your brain and the screen. There's no filter. If you type it, they have it. The only way out is using your phone's built-in keyboard.
