Shadow Profiles

Facebook builds profiles on people who DON'T have accounts — from others' contact lists, photos, tags. You're profiled without ever signing up.

What is it?

Social networks collect data about non-users. When your friend uploads their contacts, your phone number goes to Facebook — even if you never signed up. Photos get face-scanned. You get a "shadow profile" without consent.
What does it collect?
  • Phone numbers from others' contact lists
  • Email addresses from friend imports
  • Face shapes from tagged and untagged photos
  • Location patterns from other people's check-ins near you
  • Relationship connections inferred from mutual contacts
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: "To help you find friends when you join."

What they don't tell you: They build a dossier on non-users to sell targeted ads the moment you sign up — or to sell even without you joining.
How much can they see?

Surprisingly detailed. They know who you talk to, where you go, what you look like — all without you agreeing to anything. Zero consent. Full surveillance.
