Search History

Google knows every question you've asked — health worries, relationship doubts, financial fears, curiosities you'd never say aloud. Years of your inner monologue, indexed.

What is it?

Every search you type into Google, Bing, or any search engine is saved permanently. It is the most honest record of your thoughts — things you would never ask another person. Health scares at 2am. Relationship doubts. Job searches while employed. Financial panic.
What does it collect?
  • Every query with exact timestamp
  • What results you clicked and how long you stayed
  • Searches you started typing but deleted
  • Voice searches (recorded audio kept on file)
  • Autocomplete suggestions you accepted
  • Your location at the time of each search
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: "To improve search results and show relevant ads."

What they don't tell you: Your searches reveal more about you than you would tell a therapist. This data is used to profile you for advertisers, can be subpoenaed in court cases, and is stored essentially forever.
How much can they see?

They know your deepest worries, secret interests, health concerns, financial situation, relationship status, career intentions, and political views. All timestamped. All searchable. Often more revealing than a diary because you search for things you would never write down.
