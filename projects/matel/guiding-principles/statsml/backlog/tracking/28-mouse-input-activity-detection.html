28. Mouse & Keyboard Activity Detection

Every website can watch your cursor move, your keys press, and your hesitations — no permission needed.

What Is It?

Every website can watch your mouse move. Every pixel. Every pause. Every click. They track how fast you type, how long you hover, and where your cursor rests.

YouTube checks for mouse movement before deciding whether to play an ad. If nobody is there, why waste an impression?

Your cursor tells them more about your attention than your clicks ever could.
What Does It Collect?
  • Exact cursor coordinates (x, y) many times per second
  • Click locations and timing
  • Scroll depth and speed
  • Hover duration on each element
  • Typing speed and rhythm between keystrokes
  • Idle time (when you stop interacting)
  • Whether the tab is in focus or background
Why Do They Collect It?

What they tell you: Find confusing buttons. Improve page layouts. Understand what users want.

What they don't tell you:

  • Prove a human is watching (not a bot)
  • Verify someone actually saw an ad
  • Build a behavioral fingerprint from your unique mouse habits
  • Detect hesitation before purchase (then show urgency messages)
  • Identify returning users by typing rhythm alone
How Much Can They See?

They replay your entire session like a video. Every wiggle, every hesitation, every abandoned form field.

They know you hovered over "cancel subscription" for 4 seconds. They know you read the price three times.

Intrusiveness: Medium. No permission needed. Every website has this ability by default. You cannot turn it off without disabling JavaScript entirely.
