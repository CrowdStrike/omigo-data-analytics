Uber, Lyft & Delivery Apps

They know your exact routes, frequent places, daily schedule, and spending. They track your location even when you're not using the app. They can rebuild your entire life pattern.

What is it?

Ride-hailing and delivery apps like Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, and Instacart. You give them GPS access to get a ride or a meal. They keep tracking long after the trip ends. They know everywhere you go.
What does it collect?
  • Every pickup and dropoff address (home, work, friends, doctors)
  • Exact routes and timing of every trip
  • How much you spend and when you spend it
  • How often you go out and what time you return
  • What you order (food preferences, alcohol, pharmacy deliveries)
  • Surge pricing behavior — how desperate you are for a ride
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: "To improve pickup accuracy and estimate arrival times."

What they don't tell you: They use your patterns for dynamic pricing — charging more when you need it most. They sell location data to advertisers. They can reconstruct your entire weekly routine from pickup and dropoff history alone.
How much can they see?

They know where you live, where you work, who you visit on weekends, what time you leave bars, how often you see your doctor, and whether you can afford surge pricing. Delivery apps also know what you eat, what you drink, and what medications you order.
