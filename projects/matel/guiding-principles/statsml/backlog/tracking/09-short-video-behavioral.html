Short Video Behavioral Tracking

TikTok and Reels measure exactly how long you watch each frame, when you rewind, and how fast you swipe.

What is it?

Short video apps don't just count views. They measure your behavior at the millisecond level. They know exactly when you lost interest, which moment made you rewatch, whether you tilted your phone, and how fast your thumb moved to swipe away.
What does it collect?
  • Exact watch time per video (to the millisecond)
  • Which second you rewatched or paused on
  • Swipe speed and direction (hesitation = interest)
  • Whether you tilted or flipped your phone
  • Time between videos (thinking time)
  • Your WiFi name and nearby Bluetooth devices
  • Which sounds made you stop scrolling
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: We show you videos you'll enjoy based on your interests.

What they don't tell you: They're training the most advanced behavior-prediction AI ever built. They map your emotional responses. They know your political views from watch patterns. The algorithm isn't serving you — you're training it.
How much can they see?

They know your attention span to the millisecond. They know what makes you sad, angry, curious, or excited — all from watch patterns. They know your daily routine, sleep schedule, and emotional state. This is the most intimate behavioral data ever collected at scale.

Intrusiveness: Maximum. Millisecond-level emotional surveillance.
