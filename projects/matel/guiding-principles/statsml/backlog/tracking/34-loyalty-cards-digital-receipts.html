Loyalty Cards & Store Apps

The discount is the bait. Your complete purchase history is the product they sell.

What is it?

Target Circle, Costco membership, CVS ExtraCare, grocery store rewards — these programs give you small discounts in exchange for linking every single purchase to your identity. Without the card, you're anonymous. With it, they know every item you've ever bought from them.
What does it collect?
  • Every item purchased (SKU-level detail)
  • Exact time and store location
  • How you respond to coupons and sales
  • What you browse vs what you buy (app users)
  • How often you visit and how long you stay
  • Whether you use the coupon they emailed (connects email to in-store behavior)
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: "To give you personalized discounts and rewards"

What they don't tell you: Target famously predicted a teen's pregnancy before her father knew — just from buying patterns (unscented lotion, supplements, cotton balls). Stores sell purchase data to CPG brands. Your "loyalty" data determines what prices and coupons YOU see vs what others see. The $2 discount costs them nothing compared to the data's value.
How much can they see?

Item-level everything. They know your diet, health conditions (OTC medicine), family size (bulk vs single), income level (brand choices), and life changes (baby items, moving supplies). When linked to your email and phone, this merges with your online profile. A $3-off coupon buys your complete purchase DNA.
