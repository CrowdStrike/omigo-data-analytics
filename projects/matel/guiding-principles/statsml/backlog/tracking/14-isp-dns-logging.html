Internet Provider Logging

Your ISP logs every website domain you visit. No opt-out. Full browsing history.

What is it?

Your internet provider sees every website you visit. Every time you type a URL or click a link, your device asks the ISP "where is this website?" That question is called a DNS lookup. Your ISP logs every single one. There is no opt-out button.
What does it collect?
  • Every domain name you visit (not the exact page, but the site)
  • Timestamps of every visit
  • How often you visit each site
  • What time you go online and offline
  • Total bandwidth used per site
  • Which devices in your home visit which sites
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: "For network security and legal compliance."

What they don't tell you: They sell anonymized browsing profiles to advertisers. They build interest categories from your sites. In many countries, they must hand over logs to government on request without a warrant.
How much can they see?

They see your entire internet life at the domain level. They know you visited a medical site, a job board, and a divorce lawyer's page — all in the same week. VPN hides the details, but they still see you connected to a VPN and when.

Intrusiveness: Very High. No consent required. No way to turn it off without a VPN.
