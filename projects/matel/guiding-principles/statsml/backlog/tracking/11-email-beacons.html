Email Tracking Pixels

Invisible images in emails that confirm opens, reveal IP, device, and time.

What is it?

A 1x1 invisible pixel image hidden in emails. When you open the email, your device loads that image from a server. That server logs the request. You never see it. You never agreed to it. It happens the instant you open the message.
What does it collect?
  • Whether you opened the email
  • When you opened it (exact timestamp)
  • Your IP address (approximate location)
  • Your device type and email client
  • How many times you re-opened it
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: "To improve email relevance."

What they don't tell you: They score how engaged you are. Frequent openers get more emails. Non-openers get re-targeted or dropped. Your behavior decides how aggressively they market to you.
How much can they see?

They know the exact second you opened an email. They know if you read it on your phone at 2am or your work computer at 9am. They can tell if you forwarded it to someone else who also opened it.

Intrusiveness: High. Silent surveillance hidden inside every marketing email.
