Cross-Device Tracking

They link your phone, tablet, laptop, and TV into one profile using shared WiFi, logins, and device fingerprints.

What is it?

Companies link all your devices into one identity graph. Your phone, laptop, tablet, work computer, and smart TV all become one "you." They do this through shared WiFi, logged-in accounts, or device fingerprints.

You sign into Gmail on your phone and your laptop. Now they know both devices belong to the same person. Same WiFi network at home? Every device on it gets linked together.
What does it collect?
  • All devices you own or use regularly
  • Which accounts are logged in on each device
  • Your home and work IP addresses
  • Timestamps showing which device you use when
  • Apps installed across all devices
  • Browsing patterns unique to each device
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: "For a seamless experience across devices."

What they don't tell you: They sell a unified view of you to advertisers. One profile is worth more than four separate ones. They see your full day: phone in bed, laptop at work, tablet on couch, TV at night.
How much can they see?

They reconstruct your entire daily routine. Wake up (phone), commute (phone), work (laptop), lunch break (phone), evening (tablet/TV). They know your household members by linking devices on the same network.

Intrusiveness: Very High. They build a full map of your life — where, when, which screen, who else is home.
