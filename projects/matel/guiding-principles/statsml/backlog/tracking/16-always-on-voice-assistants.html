Voice Assistants: Always Listening

Alexa, Siri, and Google Assistant keep the microphone on 24/7. Accidental recordings go to cloud servers. Human reviewers listen to them.

What is it?

Voice assistants sit in your home always listening for a wake word. The microphone is always on. When it thinks it hears "Alexa" or "Hey Siri," it starts recording and sends that audio to cloud servers. Sometimes it mishears and records conversations you never meant to share.
What does it collect?
  • Audio recordings of your voice commands
  • Accidental recordings from false wake-word triggers
  • Who is speaking (voice profiles for each family member)
  • Background sounds in your home
  • Timestamps of when you're home and active
  • What questions you ask (health, relationships, finances)
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: "To process your request and improve accuracy."

What they don't tell you: Human contractors listen to recordings to grade them. Your voice data trains their AI models forever. They build a profile of your interests from questions asked. Some recordings are kept indefinitely.
How much can they see?

They hear arguments, private conversations, children playing, TV in the background. They know your daily routine by when you ask for weather or set alarms. They know your health concerns from questions asked. They know when you're home alone versus with guests from voice profiles.
