Share Link Tracking

When you share a video link with a friend, the URL secretly carries your identity, watch history, and session data.

What is it?

When you tap "Share" and send a link to a friend, that URL isn't just a link to a video. It's packed with encoded data — your user ID, how long you watched, what you watched before, your device info. When your friend opens it, the platform knows exactly who sent it.
What does it collect?
  • Your user ID encoded in the link
  • Which video triggered the share
  • Your full session before sharing
  • Your friend's identity when they open it
  • Which messaging app you used to share
  • The social connection between you two
  • Time and location of both share and open
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: It makes sharing easy and helps your friend find the right video.

What they don't tell you: It maps your real social network (not just in-app friends). It measures your influence. It tests whether content is "share-worthy." It builds a graph of who trusts whose recommendations.
How much can they see?

They see through your private messages without reading them. Every shared link reveals: who you actually talk to, what you think is worth sharing, who acts on your recommendations. You're mapping real-world relationships for them — for free.

Intrusiveness: Very High. Turns you into an unwitting data collector on your friends.
