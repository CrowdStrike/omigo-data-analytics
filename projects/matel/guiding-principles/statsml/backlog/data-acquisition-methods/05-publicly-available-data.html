Publicly Available Data

An enormous amount of data about people, places, companies, and machines is publicly readable — grouped here by why it is public: legal mandate, government publication, broadcast physics, open imagery, market disclosure, volunteered content, and research corpora.

LEGAL MANDATE

1. Public-by-Legal-Mandate Records

Deeds, land registries, court records, business filings, voter rolls, licenses — public because openness is what makes the legal function work. Nordic countries even publish individual tax figures.

property courts registries
GOVERNMENT

2. Government Statistical & Open-Data Portals

Census bureaus, national statistics offices, economic series, weather, health, crime — governments publishing aggregates and portals by policy, worldwide.

census FRED / World Bank open portals
BROADCAST

3. Live Sensor & Broadcast Feeds

Aircraft, ships, transit, earthquakes, webcams — public because the signal is literally in the air, and anyone with an antenna or an API key can listen.

ADS-B AIS GTFS
IMAGERY

4. Imagery & Mapping

Street View and its global equivalents, free satellite programs like Landsat and Sentinel, OpenStreetMap, crowdsourced street-level photos — the surface of the planet, browsable.

street view satellite OSM
MARKET

5. Corporate & Market Data Exhaust

Stock prices and filings, crypto ledgers public by design, job postings, WHOIS and certificate transparency logs, prices and reviews — commerce leaves a publicly browsable trail.

filings blockchains cert logs
VOLUNTEERED

6. User-Generated & Crowdsourced Data

Public social posts, Wikipedia, reviews, public code activity, fitness heatmaps, wardriving maps — volunteered by individuals, aggregated into datasets nobody individually consented to.

social Strava heatmap WiGLE
RESEARCH

7. Scientific & Research Corpora

Paper archives, genomic databases, public DNA matching, web crawls, astronomy surveys, the archived web — published to advance science, reused far beyond it.
