Cognitive Biases in Data Analysis

How human psychology leads analysts astray — and how automated pipelines can defend against it.

The meta-principle: Every bias is a shortcut that works most of the time but fails catastrophically in specific situations. Statistical analysis is exactly the domain where these shortcuts fail most — because data is counter-intuitive by nature. The pipeline's job is to be the adult in the room: no excitement, no stories, just numbers and thresholds.

EXPOSURE

19. Manufactured Reference Frame

When curated recommendation exposure determines what a person believes is normal, typical, or expected — without awareness that the reference was externally imposed. Differs from classical anchoring: volume-based, unconscious accumulation, not a single salient number.
EXPOSURE

20. Exposure Bias

Treating what you observed as representative of the whole. You cannot unsee a failure — so you fix it, regardless of size. The invisible problems accumulate. Good practice: always quantify how big a failure class is before fixing it.
HEURISTIC

21. Newer, Bigger, Pricier

The price tag gets read as a rating of the product, and a new model number does the same job. But the seller writes the price, and can raise it without touching what is in the box — two cameras with the same specs, one $270 dearer.
AI INTERACTION

22. Attachment to AI Personas

Affection, attraction, and attachment form toward AI-generated content that fakes human expressions and emotions. The simulated warmth re-anchors expectations — real human interaction starts to feel slow, critical, and unrewarding by comparison.
AI INTERACTION

23. The Judgment-Free Chatbot

People speak more freely to a chatbot than to humans — no social cost, no political-correctness tax, no reputation at stake. That freedom is genuinely valuable and also shifts disclosure: what people tell machines diverges from what they tell people, and data from each measures a different person.
MEASUREMENT

24. Survey Position Bias

Ask people to vote for the best song and the winner depends on the order the songs were listed in. Voters read from the top, get bored, and pick early. Nobody is lying — the ballot is doing the choosing.
HEURISTIC

25. Familiarity Feels Like Quality

Mere exposure makes the familiar feel higher-quality than it is. Dogfooding teams are the most over-trained users on earth, so their gut read of a redesign measures their own retraining cost, not the design. Mirror image of newer, bigger, pricier.
