Programming Paradigms

Different ways of thinking about how a program should be told what to do — each one changes how you write, read, and reason about code.

Ways of Telling the Computer

Data-Oriented Styles

DATA-ORIENTED STYLES

5. Vectorized Thinking

Operate on whole columns of numbers at once instead of looping over them one value at a time.

arrays no loops numpy-style
DATA-ORIENTED STYLES

6. Dataflow & Pipelines

Chain small stages so data flows from one step to the next, like items moving along an assembly line.

stages chaining ETL
DATA-ORIENTED STYLES

7. MapReduce: Split, Work, Combine

Divide a huge job across many workers, let each handle its piece, then merge the partial answers.

divide & conquer parallel big data
DATA-ORIENTED STYLES

8. Immutability

Never change data in place — make a fresh copy with the change, so nothing silently shifts under you.

no in-place edits copies safe sharing

Reacting to the World

REACTING TO THE WORLD

9. Event-Driven Programming

The program sits idle until something happens — a click, a message, a sensor reading — then responds.

events handlers triggers

10. Reactive Patterns

11. Polling vs Push

Keep asking "anything new yet?" on a timer, or wait quietly and let the source notify you when there is.
