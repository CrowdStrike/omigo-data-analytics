Healthcare & Clinical — Distribution Patterns

Hospital Length-of-Stay Extreme Skew + Spike

Days in hospital exhibit extreme right skew with a structural spike at day 1 from outpatient observation stays. The distribution is shaped by DRG coding incentives and readmission penalty windows.

  • 30% spike at day 1 (observation/outpatient coding)
  • Bulk of inpatient stays follow exponential decay
  • Long tail from ICU, rehab, and complex cases (15-60 days)
  • Pitfall: Mean LOS is meaningless — pulled right by outliers. Median or geometric mean required.
  • DRG reimbursement creates artificial clustering at threshold days
  • Readmission penalties (30-day window) shape discharge timing
Blood Pressure (Systolic) Bimodal

Population-level systolic BP is bimodal: a healthy cluster around 118 mmHg and a hypertensive cluster at 145+. A single normal assumption misses both sub-populations.

  • Healthy peak ~118 mmHg (70% of population)
  • Hypertensive shoulder at 145 mmHg (treated + untreated)
  • Severe hypertension tail extending past 160 mmHg
  • Pitfall: Treatment thresholds (140/90) create measurement artifacts — patients cluster just below cutoffs.
  • A single Gaussian fit overestimates variance and misses the gap
  • Gaussian mixture models (k=2 or 3) are the correct approach
Drug Response Time Zero-Inflated Log-Normal

Time-to-response for a drug follows log-normal kinetics (multiplicative biological processes), but 25% of patients are non-responders who never respond — creating a spike at zero.

  • 25% non-responders (zero/no response) — distinct population
  • Responders follow log-normal: pharmacokinetics is multiplicative
  • Median response ~12 hours, but heavy right tail
  • Pitfall: Intention-to-treat includes non-responders (dilutes effect). Per-protocol excludes them (inflates effect).
  • Zero-inflated models or hurdle models required
  • Standard t-test on raw data is invalid — mixture of point mass + continuous
White Blood Cell Count Right Shoulder

WBC in a mixed clinical population shows a primary healthy peak at 7 x10^3/uL with a right shoulder from active infections, and a sparse extreme tail from leukemia/sepsis.

  • Healthy reference range: 4.5-11 x10^3/uL (80% of samples)
  • Infection shoulder at 12 x10^3/uL (15%)
  • Extreme values 20-40 from leukemia, severe sepsis (5%)
  • Pitfall: Reference ranges assume normality — but WBC is right-skewed even in healthy subjects.
  • Log-transform yields approximate normality for statistical testing
  • Clinical decision thresholds interact with the distribution shape
Time Between ER Visits Bursty + Periodic

Inter-arrival times at the ER combine exponential waiting (Poisson assumption) with periodic spikes from Monday mornings (weekend injuries) and Friday nights (alcohol-related).

  • Base inter-arrival: exponential with rate ~0.1 (mean 10 hours)
  • Monday morning surge: spike around 2 hours between visits
  • Friday night surge: clustered arrivals around 120-hour mark in weekly cycle
  • Pitfall: Poisson process assumes constant rate — periodic surges violate this fundamentally.
  • Non-homogeneous Poisson or Hawkes process needed
  • Staffing models based on constant-rate assumption under-resource peak times
