Car Dealerships — Distribution Patterns

5 distributions that expose the hidden mechanics of car pricing, lot economics, and buyer behavior

DESPERATION SPIKE

Discount by Day (Month-End Push)

Discount percentage follows a hockey stick — flat 3-5% discount days 1-20, then exponential ramp to 12-18% discount days 25-31. Salespeople chasing monthly quotas will give away margin they'd never concede on day 5.

  • Days 1-20: steady 3-5% discount (take-it-or-leave-it)
  • Days 25-31: panic discounts up to 18%
  • End-of-quarter (day 31 of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec) = maximum desperation
  • Same car, same dealer, 3x discount — just wait
CLEARANCE CLIFF

Model-Year (Cliff When New Arrives)

Bimodal: dealers hold price for months, then a massive clearance cliff when the new model year is announced. The car didn't change — but the NUMBER on the sticker did, and that's enough to destroy 15% of value overnight.

  • Holding phase: discount hovers around 4% (months)
  • Clearance phase: discount jumps to 22% mean in final 6 weeks
  • The car is physically identical the day before and after announcement
  • Depreciation is socially constructed, not physical
LOT AGING PRESSURE

Lot Age (Weibull Survival — Aging = Pressure)

Days on lot follows a Weibull distribution (k=1.8, lambda=45). Most sell in 30-60 days, but a long tail sits on the lot bleeding floor plan interest. Every day past 60 costs the dealer $25-50 in financing — they're paying to keep the car.

  • 0-30 days: fresh stock, full margin, no pressure
  • 30-60 days: floor plan interest eating into profit
  • 60+ days: dealer is LOSING money holding this car
  • Floor plan interest = the hidden clock ticking on every car
RATE SENSITIVITY

Interest Rate Impact (Demand Shift)

When rates go from 3% to 7%, the entire demand distribution shifts left. Same cars, same incomes — but monthly payments become unaffordable. The distribution shape doesn't change, it just MOVES. That's a location shift, not a shape change.

  • Low rates (3%): mean 65 units/month per dealer
  • High rates (7%): mean 42 units/month per dealer
  • 1% rate increase = ~$50/month payment increase
  • 8% demand drop per percentage point of rate
INCENTIVE STACKING

Stacked Incentives (EV + Mfr + Dealer)

Total discount is a sum of multiple independent incentive distributions. The sticker price is fiction — the REAL price is determined by which combination of incentives you qualify for. The resulting distribution is multimodal with spikes at common stack totals.

  • Base dealer discount: uniform $1K-$3K
  • Manufacturer rebate: 40% chance of ~$3K
  • EV tax credit: 30% chance of $7,500
  • Loyalty/trade-in bonus: ~$1,500
  • Total can range from $2K to $16K+ off MSRP
