Foot Traffic & In-Store Conversion — Distribution Patterns

5 distributions that reveal how physical retail behavior shapes revenue before any product decision is made

COMPOUND LOSS

Entry→Purchase (Geometric Decay)

Each stage loses 50% of traffic. Enter(1000) → Browse(500) → Try(250) → Buy(125). The geometric distribution of "stage reached" shows compounding losses — fix ANY single stage for 2x revenue at the bottom.

  • Stage 1 (Enter): 1000 customers
  • Stage 2 (Browse): 500 (−50%)
  • Stage 3 (Try): 250 (−50%)
  • Stage 4 (Buy): 125 (−50%) = 12.5% final conversion
NOBODY IS AVERAGE

Time in Store (Bimodal)

Two distinct populations: quick-trip shoppers (returns, pickup — exponential mean 4 min) and serious shoppers (normal, mean 35 min). The "average" of 15 min describes literally nobody in the store.

  • Quick trip: N=1200, exponential mean=4 min
  • Serious shop: N=800, normal mean=35, sd=8
  • The 15-min "average" is a statistical ghost
  • Two service models needed, not one
STAFF TO THE CURVE

Conversion by Hour (Bell Curve)

Conversion peaks at lunch (12pm) and after-work (5:30pm) — two overlapping normals. But staff scheduling is flat or mismatched, leaving revenue on the table at peaks and wasted labor at troughs.

  • Peak 1: lunch rush, mean=12, sd=1.5
  • Peak 2: after-work, mean=17.5, sd=1.2
  • Staff schedule rarely matches demand curve
  • Understaffed peaks = lost conversions
DIFFUSION OF RESPONSIBILITY

Group Size (Monotone Decreasing)

Solo shoppers convert at 38%. Each additional person in the group drops conversion — pairs 28%, triples 18%, groups of 6 just 5%. Social diffusion: "someone else will decide."

  • Solo = 38% conversion (highest)
  • Pair = 28%, Triple = 18%
  • Group of 4 = 12%, 5 = 8%, 6 = 5%
  • Sales approach must change by group size
STRONGEST PREDICTOR

Weather → Traffic Shift (Conditional)

Rain pushes traffic INTO malls (mean=180) while sunny days pull it outside (mean=120). Weather is the strongest uncontrolled traffic predictor — better than any promo campaign.

  • Rain day: mall traffic UP, normal mean=180, sd=30
  • Sunny day: mall traffic DOWN, normal mean=120, sd=25
  • Street retail inverts: rain −40%, sun +30%
  • Weather = strongest uncontrolled predictor
