Restaurant & Fast Food Analytics — Distribution Patterns

Distributions that reveal how menu psychology, timing, and UI design shape every transaction

MENU QUADRANTS

Menu Engineering — Popularity × Margin

50 menu items classified by popularity and margin into 4 quadrants. The position on the menu determines the quadrant — not food quality. Stars get prime placement, Dogs get buried or removed.

  • Stars (high pop, high margin) — promote aggressively
  • Plowhorses (high pop, low margin) — raise price or reduce cost
  • Puzzles (low pop, high margin) — better placement/description
  • Dogs (low pop, low margin) — remove or redesign
TWO DECISION MODES

Order Decision Time (Bimodal)

Two distinct populations: quick deciders who already know what they want (regulars, habitual orderers) and deliberators who read everything. Menu design targets the deliberators — they spend 40% more per visit.

  • Quick deciders: < 30s, regulars, habitual
  • Deliberators: 3-5 min, read everything, explore
  • Deliberators spend 40% more per transaction
  • "Average" decision time describes neither group
VARIANCE = REVENUE

Table Turnover (Erlang)

Fast food table times follow a tight, predictable pattern — most groups finish within a narrow window. Fine dining has wide variance making revenue unpredictable. The revenue model minimizes variance, not time.

  • Fast food: Erlang(k=3, λ=0.05) → tight peak ~60 min
  • Fine dining: Erlang(k=2, λ=0.015) → 90-180 min spread
  • Revenue = minimize variance, not minimize time
  • Predictability enables capacity planning
ANCHORING UPSELL

Combo vs À La Carte (Anchoring Effect)

À la carte orders cluster at $8.50 while combos cluster at $12. Combo buyers spend 40% more than they would ordering only what they need. Savings are computed against inflated à la carte prices.

  • À la carte mean: $8.50 (what you actually need)
  • Combo mean: $12.00 (bundled with extras)
  • +$5 for items you didn't originally want
  • Savings framing: compared to inflated reference prices
UI-CREATED SHAPE

Tip Distribution (Trimodal — UI Created)

The tip distribution has spikes at exactly 15%, 18%, and 20% — not because of social norms, but because those are the three buttons on the iPad screen. Change the defaults and the distribution changes instantly.

  • Spikes at 15%, 18%, 20% = the three buttons
  • Zero-tip cluster = takeout orders (no prompt)
  • < 3% of users tap "Custom"
  • Change defaults → change distribution instantly
