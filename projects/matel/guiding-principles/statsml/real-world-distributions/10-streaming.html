Streaming & Recommendations — Distribution Patterns

POWER LAW (CONCENTRATED)

Play Counts (Long Tail Theory Is Wrong)

Top 1% of content gets 90% of plays, power law decay, millions of tracks with <100 plays ever. The "long tail" theory (niche content thrives online) is disproved by this shape. Recommendation algorithms amplify concentration rather than spreading it.

  • Top 1% = 90% of all plays
  • Millions of tracks with <100 plays lifetime
  • "Long tail" theory debunked — algorithms amplify concentration
  • Power law steeper on Spotify than in physical record stores
BATHTUB + SPIKES

Time to Abandon (The 2-Minute Hook Window)

High dropout in first 2 minutes, stable middle, second rise at episode end, spike at "skip intro" timestamp. The 2-minute window is the hook threshold Netflix designs cold opens for. You have 120 seconds to capture attention — not gradual decline, binary judgment.

  • First 2 min = binary "stay or leave" decision
  • Stable middle = committed viewers
  • Spike at intro = those without auto-skip
  • 120 seconds = the designed hook window
LEFT-SKEWED (BOUNDED)

Genre Diversity (Cognitive Ceiling of ~5)

Most users cluster at 3-5 genres consumed, hard wall at 8-10. Not algorithm failure — it's a cognitive ceiling. Humans maintain interest in ~5 domains regardless of unlimited catalog. The wall exists in every platform regardless of diversity interventions.

  • Most users: 3-5 genres (tight cluster)
  • Hard ceiling at 8-10 regardless of catalog size
  • Not algorithm failure — cognitive limit
  • Wall exists on every platform regardless of diversity efforts
GEOMETRIC + HEAVY TAIL

Binge Length (Cliffhanger Effect Measurable)

Geometric decay (50% watch 1, 25% watch 2...) but tail is fatter than predicted — 5+ episode sessions happen way more than coin-flip model predicts. The excess tail = cliffhanger effect. You can quantify "addictiveness" as gap between observed tail and geometric prediction.

  • Geometric body = constant "stop?" probability per episode
  • Excess tail = cliffhanger effect (compulsion to continue)
  • Gap between observed and geometric = addictiveness metric
  • Shows without hooks have perfect geometric decay
UI-DEPENDENT SHAPE

Rating Distribution (UI Design Artifact)

5-star systems produce J-curves. Binary thumbs = 90%+ positive. 10-point scales = trimodal (1,7,10). The rating system creates its own distribution — Netflix switched from 5-star to thumbs because J-curve made 3-star content invisible.

  • 5-star → J-curve (spikes at 1 and 5)
  • Binary thumbs → 90%+ positive
  • 10-point → trimodal (1, 7, 10)
  • Shape is artifact of UI, not content quality
