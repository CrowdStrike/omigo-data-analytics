Crypto & DeFi — Distribution Patterns

CAUCHY-LIKE (UNDEFINED MOMENTS)

Token Returns (Mean and Variance Don't Exist)

Similar to stock returns but tails are so fat that mean and variance don't converge — add more data and estimates keep jumping. Any risk metric using standard deviation is meaningless. Sharpe ratio, VaR, portfolio optimization — all mathematically invalid for crypto.

  • Tails so fat mean/variance never converge
  • More data → estimates keep jumping (no law of large numbers)
  • Sharpe ratio, VaR = mathematically invalid
  • Standard portfolio optimization is broken by construction
EXTREME POWER LAW

Wallet Balances (More Concentrated Than Traditional Finance)

Power law steeper than wealth inequality — top 0.01% hold 30-50% of supply. Directly contradicts the "democratization" narrative. The exponent quantifies exactly how centralized a "decentralized" system is.

  • Top 0.01% hold 30-50% of supply
  • Steeper concentration than traditional finance
  • Directly contradicts decentralization narrative
  • Power law exponent = centralization metric
BIMODAL (TWO MARKETS)

DEX Swap Sizes (Retail Provides Exit Liquidity for Whales)

Spike at $50-$500 (retail), dead zone, second mass at $100K+ (whales/MEV bots). Two completely separate markets sharing one pool. Price impact is dominated by the right mode — retail is just providing exit liquidity.

  • Spike at $50-$500 (retail traders)
  • Second mass at $100K+ (whales, MEV bots)
  • Dead zone proves two separate markets in one pool
  • "Average swap size" describes nobody
PHASE TRANSITION

Gas Fees (Stable Until Capacity, Then Explosion)

Normal times = tight cluster around base fee. Congestion = exponential tail reaching 100× normal. Not gradual — it's a phase transition. EIP-1559 creates stable pricing until utilization hits ~100%, then the auction mechanism kicks in.

  • Tight cluster in normal operation (stable pricing)
  • Phase transition at ~100% utilization
  • Exponential explosion to 100× during congestion
  • EIP-1559 mechanism creates the cliff (not gradual)
ZERO-INFLATED + FLOOR SPIKE

NFT Prices (Floor Spike = Dead Collection)

Spike at floor price (people dumping to exit), log-normal body for "real" trades, occasional extreme outlier. The floor price spike mass diagnoses project health — living collections have smooth log-normal, dead collections have 90% at floor.

  • Floor price spike = distressed selling (illiquidity)
  • Log-normal body = genuine price discovery
  • Spike mass / total = collection health metric
  • Dead project: 90% at floor. Living project: smooth log-normal
