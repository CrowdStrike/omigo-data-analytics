Poisson Distribution — Real-World Examples

Events occurring independently at a constant average rate. The key property: mean = variance. When variance > mean, the data is overdispersed and Poisson no longer fits.

Spatial Process

Raindrops Per Square Meter (λ=8)

Count of drops hitting a 1m² surface in 10 seconds during steady rain.

  • Rain intensity is approximately constant during steady precipitation
  • Spatial Poisson process: each small sub-area has equal probability of receiving a drop
  • Independence of drops — one landing does not affect where the next falls
  • Breaks down during gusts or variable intensity storms
Sports Analytics

Goals Per Soccer Match (λ=2.7)

Total goals scored across top-flight leagues per match.

  • Scoring attempts are many, probability of each converting is low
  • Goals are roughly independent events within a match
  • Slight overdispersion from team quality differences (strong vs weak teams)
  • Used extensively in sports betting models
Classic Example

Typos Per Page of Text (λ=1.5)

Proofreading data: errors per standard page of typed text.

  • Classic Poisson example (Ladislaus Bortkiewicz, 1898)
  • Each word has a small independent chance of containing an error
  • Many words per page, tiny probability per word — Poisson regime
  • Fatigue effects over long documents can violate stationarity
Queueing Theory

Customers Arriving at a Bakery (λ=12 per 10min)

Arrivals during a 10-minute window mid-morning.

  • Arrivals approximately independent — one customer does not cause another
  • Rate roughly constant within a narrow time window
  • Varies by hour (non-homogeneous Poisson across the day)
  • Foundation for M/M/1 and M/M/c queueing models
Genetics

Mutations Per Gene Per Generation (λ=0.7)

Number of point mutations in a gene across one generation.

  • Each nucleotide has a tiny independent mutation probability
  • Gene has many nucleotides — sum approximates Poisson
  • Hotspots violate the independence assumption (clustered mutations)
  • Used in molecular clock calibration and neutral evolution models
Rare Events

Meteorite Impacts Per Century (λ=3)

Number of significant meteorite impacts (>1m diameter) on Earth per century.

  • Memoryless process — no clustering expected for independent objects
  • Each moment has a tiny probability of impact, time horizon is large
  • Size distribution is separate (follows Pareto / power law)
  • Low λ means high probability of zero-event centuries
