Climate & Weather — Distribution Patterns

ZERO-INFLATED GAMMA

Daily Rainfall (Average Is Dangerously Misleading)

60-70% of days are exactly 0mm (no rain). Among rainy days: gamma distribution (right-skewed, mode at light rain, tail to heavy events). "Average rainfall" is meaningless because it mixes dry and wet days. A city with 1000mm/year could get it in 50 storms or 200 drizzles — wildly different flood risk. Shape tells you drought-vs-deluge risk, mean tells you nothing.

  • 60-70% of days = exactly 0mm (zero-inflated)
  • Rainy days follow gamma (right-skewed)
  • "Average daily rainfall" mixes two populations (dry + wet)
  • Shape (zero ratio + tail weight) predicts flood risk better than mean
BIMODAL (PHASE TRANSITION)

Hurricane Intensity (Phase Transition, Not Gradual)

Cluster at Category 1-2, gap, second cluster at Category 4-5. Fewer moderate Cat 3 than either extreme. The gap proves rapid intensification is not gradual strengthening — storms either fizzle or trigger a phase transition and explode. Warming may not make storms "slightly worse" but increase the probability of triggering the jump.

  • Cluster at Cat 1-2 (weak storms that fizzle)
  • Gap at Cat 3 (unstable transition zone)
  • Cluster at Cat 4-5 (rapidly intensified monsters)
  • Climate change may increase phase-transition probability, not gradual shift
VARIANCE EXPANSION

Temperature Anomaly (Variance Change > Mean)

Each year approximately Gaussian, but spread is widening — more extreme cold AND hot days. "Global warming" as mean shift is the lesser story. Variance expansion means record colds still happen even as mean rises — confuses the public ("it was freezing!") but is consistent with wider distribution. Shape change matters more than location change.

  • Each year: approximately Gaussian anomaly
  • Variance widening over decades (more extremes both ways)
  • Record cold still happens = wider distribution, not contradicting warming
  • Shape change (spread) > location change (mean shift)
WEIBULL (k≈2)

Wind Speed (Shape Parameter = Financial Model)

Rises from zero, peaks at moderate speed, decays. Never negative. The Weibull fit is so consistent globally that wind farm revenue = integral of Weibull CDF above turbine cut-in speed. A 10% error in shape parameter k = millions in miscalculated revenue over 25-year turbine life. The shape parameter IS the financial model.

  • Weibull with k≈2 (Rayleigh special case) fits globally
  • Wind farm revenue = integral above cut-in speed
  • 10% error in k = millions $ miscalculated over 25 years
  • Shape parameter IS the financial projection
POWER LAW (SCALE-INVARIANT)

Earthquake Magnitude (No Maximum, Scale Invariant)

Perfect linear relationship in log(frequency) vs magnitude. Every +1 magnitude = 10× fewer events. Astonishingly consistent worldwide. Scale-invariant: same physics produces M2 and M9, just at different rates. No "maximum possible" in the distribution. "Overdue for the big one" is wrong — power law is memoryless.

  • Log-linear: each +1 magnitude = 10× fewer events
  • Scale-invariant (same physics at all scales)
  • No maximum possible earthquake in the distribution
  • Power law is memoryless — "overdue" reasoning is wrong
