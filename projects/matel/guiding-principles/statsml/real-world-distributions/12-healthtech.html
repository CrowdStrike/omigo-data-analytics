Health Tech & Wearables — Distribution Patterns

CONDITIONAL BIMODAL

Sleep Duration (Alarm Compresses Variation)

Weekday = tight bell at 6.5-7 hrs (alarm-forced). Weekend = bimodal with peaks at 7 hrs (habitual) and 9-10 hrs (catch-up). The weekday tightness proves sleep is externally constrained, not biologically chosen. Weekend bimodality reveals two populations: set rhythm vs chronic deficit.

  • Weekday: tight Gaussian at 6.5-7 hrs (alarm-constrained)
  • Weekend: bimodal — habitual (7 hrs) + catch-up (9-10 hrs)
  • Tightness = external constraint, not biological choice
  • Bimodal weekend split reveals chronic sleep debt population
NORMAL + JUMPS

Resting Heart Rate (Shape = Illness Signal)

Stable Gaussian day-to-day, but mean drifts down with fitness. Sudden 5+ BPM jumps = illness onset. Jump detection is more valuable than absolute number. A 58→65 jump in one person = stronger signal than stable 72 in another. Wearables detecting distribution shift can flag COVID 2 days before symptoms.

  • Stable Gaussian day-to-day (low variance)
  • Mean drifts down slowly with fitness gains
  • Sudden jumps (5+ BPM) = illness onset signal
  • Distribution shift detection > absolute threshold
BIMODAL

Daily Steps (Two Populations, No Middle Ground)

Spike at ~2000 steps (sedentary desk worker minimum), gap, second hill at 8000-12000 (active movers). The bimodal shape proves two populations exist. The gap (4000-6000) is unstable — people fall to sedentary or climb to active. Very few sustain the middle.

  • Spike at ~2000 = sedentary minimum (desk + bathroom)
  • Second hill at 8000-12000 = active movers
  • Gap at 4000-6000 = unstable zone (nobody stays here)
  • Two basins of attraction, not a spectrum
SHAPE TRANSITION

Blood Glucose (Shape Precedes Diagnosis by Years)

Healthy = tight symmetric bell (80-120 mg/dL). Pre-diabetic = same bell with post-meal right shoulder appearing. Diabetic = right tail dominates. Shape transition from symmetric → right-shouldered → right-dominated IS disease progression. Detectable years before clinical diagnosis.

  • Healthy: tight symmetric bell (80-120 mg/dL)
  • Pre-diabetic: right shoulder appears (post-meal spikes)
  • Diabetic: right tail dominates the shape
  • Shape change precedes clinical diagnosis by years
LOG-NORMAL + CLASS SPIKES

Workout Duration (Most Exercise Is Externally Timed)

Spikes at 30, 45, 60 minutes (class-driven), log-normal body underneath for free exercise. Spikes reveal most exercise is externally timed (class schedules, app workouts). When a spike at 7 minutes appears, that's the "gave up" population — larger than anyone admits.

  • Spikes at 30, 45, 60 min = class schedule times
  • Log-normal body = people who listen to their body
  • Spike at 7 min = "gave up" population (hidden in self-reported data)
  • Most exercise is externally timed, not internally motivated
CIRCADIAN SINUSOIDAL + NOISE

Body Temperature (Circadian Rhythm Hidden in Noise)

Looks Gaussian at 98.6°F if you measure once daily. Continuous monitoring reveals a sinusoidal signal (low at 4am, peak at 6pm, amplitude ~1°F) buried under 0.3°F random noise. The distribution shape depends entirely on WHEN you sample — one measurement/day aliases a wave into a bell.

  • Single measurement/day → appears Gaussian (aliasing artifact)
  • Continuous → sinusoidal with ~1°F amplitude
  • Trough at 4am, peak at 6pm (circadian signal)
  • Fever detection requires knowing phase, not just threshold
ZERO-INFLATED POISSON

Stress Episodes Per Day (Most Days = Zero Signal)

60% of days register zero elevated-stress events. Remaining days follow a Poisson-like count (1-3 events typical, rare 5+ days). The zero inflation proves wearable stress detection has a binary first stage: "something happened vs nothing." The non-zero days cluster on work days.

  • 60% at zero = most days, nothing triggers the sensor
  • Non-zero days: Poisson with λ ≈ 2
  • Binary first stage: event vs no-event detection
  • 5+ events/day = burnout marker (rare but predictive)
WEIBULL SURVIVAL

App Engagement Duration (Weibull Survival Decay)

Health app engagement follows Weibull decay with shape k < 1 (decreasing hazard). 40% abandon within week 1, another 25% by week 4. Survivors past 6 weeks rarely quit — they've formed habits. The decreasing hazard rate means: if you survived this long, you're increasingly likely to stay.

  • 40% gone in week 1 (novelty exhausted)
  • 25% more by week 4 (habit never formed)
  • Survivors past 6 weeks = habit locked in
  • Decreasing hazard rate = survival predicts survival
MISLEADING COMPOSITE

Sleep Score (Composite Metric, Personal Baseline Matters)

Unlike steps or heart rate (directly measured by sensor), sleep score is a computed abstraction — a proprietary blend of duration, stages, HR, and movement with opaque, unvalidated weights. It's not an exact science. Looks Gaussian at population level (mean ~75), but cross-user comparison is meaningless. User A's range is 68-80, User B's is 55-72. Only YOUR delta from YOUR baseline carries signal — the absolute number is marketing, not medicine.

  • NOT directly measured — unlike steps, HR, SpO2
  • Proprietary composite: weights are arbitrary, not clinically validated
  • Same score = different meaning per user (baselines vary wildly)
  • Only useful signal: personal delta from your own rolling mean
