**Page type:** grid page (tutorials category grid: single flat 4-column nav-grid of cards with topic tags)
**HTML title tag:** S3 & Object Storage Deep Dive

**Subtitle:** One name, one HTTP call, one flat map from names to bytes — and then the details that decide whether a bucket serves a huge dataset smoothly or slows to a crawl: spreading load across names, consistency, replication, and who is allowed to read a byte.
