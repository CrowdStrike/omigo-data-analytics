Server-Side Logging

Every time you visit a website, the server writes down who you are, when you came, and what you looked at.

What is it?

The website's own diary. Every visit gets recorded automatically. No extra software needed. The server just writes it down like a guest book you never see.
What does it collect?
  • Your IP address (gives away your rough location)
  • Exact time you visited, down to the second
  • Which page you opened
  • What browser and device you use
  • Where you came from (the link that sent you here)
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: We need it to fix errors and keep the site running smoothly.

What they don't tell you: They sell aggregated traffic patterns. They use your location for price discrimination. They share logs with ad networks.
How much can they see?

They see every single page load. They know your general city. They know if you came from Google or a competitor's link. But they can't tell exactly who you are without combining it with other data.

Intrusiveness: Low-Medium. Always on. No way to opt out.
