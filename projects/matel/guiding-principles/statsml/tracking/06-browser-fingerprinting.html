Browser Fingerprinting

They identify you without cookies by combining your screen size, fonts, and browser quirks into a unique ID.

What is it?

A technique that identifies your specific computer by combining dozens of small details. Your screen resolution, installed fonts, graphics card behavior, timezone, language settings. Together these create a fingerprint as unique as a real one. You can't delete it because nothing is stored on your device.
What does it collect?
  • Your screen size and color depth
  • List of installed fonts
  • Graphics card rendering patterns
  • Timezone and language
  • Browser plugins and settings
  • How your device draws invisible test images
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: It helps prevent fraud and detect bots.

What they don't tell you: It's designed to track you even after you clear cookies, use private browsing, or switch accounts. It's the backup plan for when you try to hide.
How much can they see?

They can identify your specific device with over 90% accuracy. Clearing cookies does nothing. Private mode does nothing. It's like a tattoo you can't wash off. Only getting a new computer resets it.

Intrusiveness: Very High. Invisible, unclearable, follows you forever.
