Phone Permission Requests

That popup asking for camera or location access — you tap Allow once, and data flows to their servers forever.

What is it?

When an app asks "Allow access to your location?" — that one tap opens a permanent pipeline. The app can now access that data whenever it wants, running in the background, sending it to their servers without asking again.
What does it collect?
  • Your exact GPS location (even when app is closed)
  • Your contacts list and call history
  • Your photos and their hidden location data
  • Microphone audio (if permitted)
  • Health data and step counts
  • Calendar events and reminders
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: Maps need location. Photo apps need camera access. It's required for the app to work.

What they don't tell you: They collect location data every few minutes even when the app is closed. They sell location histories to data brokers. They scan your contacts to build social graphs of everyone you know.
How much can they see?

Once you grant permission, they have a permanent key. Location means they know where you sleep, work, worship, and who you visit. Contacts means they map your entire social network. Photos means they scan every image on your phone.

Intrusiveness: Extreme. One tap = permanent access to intimate data.
