JavaScript Tags

Invisible scripts on web pages that watch everything you do — every click, scroll, and hesitation.

What is it?

Tiny programs that run inside your browser when you visit a page. They watch your mouse, record your clicks, measure how far you scroll. The page looks normal. But these scripts are quietly reporting everything back.
What does it collect?
  • Every click and tap
  • Scroll depth and speed
  • How long you hover over something
  • Form fields you type in (even if you don't submit)
  • Rage clicks (frustrated repeated clicking)
  • Mouse movement patterns
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: It helps us improve the website experience for you.

What they don't tell you: They replay your entire session like a surveillance video. They sell behavioral profiles. They detect when you're about to leave and trigger pop-ups.
How much can they see?

Everything you do on the page. In real time. Every hesitation, every deleted keystroke, every abandoned cart. It's like someone watching over your shoulder while you browse.

Intrusiveness: High. Records behavior at millisecond level.
