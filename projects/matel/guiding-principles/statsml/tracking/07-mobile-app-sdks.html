Mobile App SDKs

Hidden code libraries inside your phone apps that quietly report your actions to analytics and ad companies.

What is it?

When a developer builds an app, they drop in pre-made code packages from companies like Facebook, Google, or Appsflyer. These packages automatically collect your data and send it back to those companies. The app developer gets free analytics. The company gets your behavior.
What does it collect?
  • Every screen you open in the app
  • Every button you tap
  • How long you spend on each screen
  • Your phone model, OS version, and carrier
  • Your advertising ID (a tracking number for your phone)
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • In-app purchases and subscription status
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: It helps developers improve their apps and measure ad performance.

What they don't tell you: These SDKs run in the background constantly. They share data across apps from different companies. They build a complete profile of your phone usage even when you think you're using just one app.
How much can they see?

They see every action inside the app. Multiple apps with the same SDK (like Facebook SDK) share data. They know you used a fitness app at 6am, a dating app at 8pm, and a banking app at noon — all from "different" apps reporting to the same company.

Intrusiveness: High. Cross-app surveillance through hidden code.
