Analytics Platforms

Tools like Google Analytics and Mixpanel that turn your website visits into dashboards, funnels, and user profiles.

What is it?

Software that companies install to track everything visitors do. It bundles individual actions into "user journeys." It shows where people come from, what they click, and where they give up and leave.
What does it collect?
  • Full session recordings (pages visited in order)
  • Time spent on each page
  • Which buttons you click
  • Where you drop off in checkout or signup
  • Your device type, browser, screen size
  • Whether you're new or returning
Why do they collect it?

What they tell you: We want to improve the product and understand what users need.

What they don't tell you: Your behavior feeds machine learning that optimizes for addiction. Anonymized data gets re-identified and sold. They know more about your habits than you do.
How much can they see?

Your entire journey across the site, session by session. They group you into segments — high-value, at-risk, new. They predict what you'll do next. They know you better than you know yourself online.

Intrusiveness: High. Builds a behavioral model of each individual user.
