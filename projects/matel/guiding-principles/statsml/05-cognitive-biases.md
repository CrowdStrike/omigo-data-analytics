# Cognitive Biases in Data Analysis

**Page type:** grid page (card navigation grid, 4 columns)
**HTML title tag:** Cognitive Biases in Data Analysis

**Subtitle:** How human psychology leads analysts astray — and how automated pipelines can defend against it.

## Callout (philosophy box)

**The meta-principle:** Every bias is a shortcut that works most of the time but fails catastrophically in specific situations. Statistical analysis is exactly the domain where these shortcuts fail most — because data is counter-intuitive by nature. The pipeline's job is to be the adult in the room: no excitement, no stories, just numbers and thresholds.
