Water/Climate - Domain-Specific Pitfalls

Pitfall Description
Chaotic systems / butterfly effect Small error in initial conditions → completely different forecast after 10 days; deterministic prediction impossible beyond 2 weeks; must use probabilistic ensembles; single-path forecast is false precision
Station network sparse Weather station every 50-100km in developed countries; every 500km in developing; ocean: almost zero; interpolating between stations: smooth but misses local topography effects; mountain vs valley 10km apart = completely different
Climate ≠ weather confusion Climate = 30-year statistics; weather = tomorrow; predicting climate change says nothing about next Tuesday; conflating the two in either direction is wrong; "it's cold today" doesn't disprove warming
Extreme event prediction from limited history 100-year flood: by definition 1% annual probability; but we have 50-70 years of records; estimating 1% events from 50 data points: massive uncertainty; the rarest events = most consequential AND least estimable
Downscaling artifacts Global climate model: 100km grid; need: 1km local prediction; statistical downscaling introduces patterns that exist in the method not in reality; bias correction can create physically impossible scenarios
Non-stationarity from climate change Engineering standards based on "historical 100-year flood level"; but climate change shifts that level; the past is no longer a guide to the future; every statistical return period is wrong if the underlying distribution is shifting
