Earthquake - Domain-Specific Pitfalls

Pitfall Description
Prediction fundamentally impossible No reliable precursor signal found in 50+ years of research; chaotic trigger mechanism; "earthquake prediction" in the seismological sense remains unsolved; probabilistic hazard ≠ prediction
Recurrence interval from n=3-5 events Major earthquake on this fault: historical record shows events at 1700, 1857, 1906; average interval = 100 years? But n=3 → CI is enormous; could be 60 years or 200; Poisson assumption may be wrong
Catalog incompleteness Pre-1970: only large earthquakes detected; seismometer network expanded → "more earthquakes!" No — better detection; apparent increase in events = instrumentation improvement not actual increase; historical comparison invalid
Aftershock vs new event M7.0 → M5.5 two days later: aftershock? Or: M5.5 = foreshock of coming M8.0? Distinction matters enormously for response; statistics help (Omori law) but individual classification is uncertain for days
Induced seismicity Fracking, wastewater injection, reservoir filling → earthquakes in previously stable areas; these aren't in historical record; hazard models based on "natural" seismicity underestimate risk in areas with human activity
Building vulnerability unknown Earthquake ground motion × building response = damage; but: actual construction quality unknown without inspection; code compliance ≠ actual construction; soft-story buildings identified post-facto after they collapse
